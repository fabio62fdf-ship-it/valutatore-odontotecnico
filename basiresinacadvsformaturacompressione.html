Rheological Stages of Powder/Liquid Mixture (Flask Compression Molding)

During mixing of liquid MMA monomer with PMMA polymer powder (1:3 volume ratio), the system passes through 5 fundamental physical stages prior to polymerization.

Stage 1

Wet / Sandy

Liquid envelops polymer beads without dissolution. Fluid and grainy appearance.

Stage 2

Viscous / Stringy

MMA penetrates PMMA beads, partially dissolving chains. Compound becomes sticky and strings.

Stage 3 • Climax

Plastic / Dough Stage

Operational packing stage! Plastic, moldable mass, non-sticky. Ready for flask pressing.

Stage 4

Rubbery / Elastic

Further monomer evaporation. Compound exhibits elastic memory and cannot be packed.

Stage 5

Rigid / Hardened

Complete surface MMA evaporation and rigid stiffness onset before thermal curing.

Compression Molding

Traditional Gypsum Flask

  • • Exothermic Reaction: Generates ~13.8 kcal/mol. Requires temperature control below 100.8 °C.
  • • Thermal Cycle: 70 °C for 9 hours or rapid cycle (70 °C for 90 min + boiling for 30 min).
  • • Mechanical Pressure: Flask clamp packing reduces shrinkage from 21% to 6-7%.

CAD/CAM Milling

HPHT Industrial Discs

  • • HPHT Synthesis: Polymerized under 100-300 MPa pressure.
  • • Conversion: Double bond conversion > 99.5%, residual monomer < 0.3%.
  • • Subtractive: Zero polymerization shrinkage during laboratory milling.

Additive 3D Printing

UV Photopolymerization

  • • Oligomers: UDMA, Bis-GMA, TEGDMA reactive at 385-405 nm.
  • • Layer-by-Layer: Sequential printing with initial conversion of 60-75%.
  • • Post-Curing: IPA wash and UV nitrogen oven curing to reach 85-90%.

Defect Diagnosis: Porosity and Microscopic Structure

Boiling Porosity

Cause: Water bath temperature raised too rapidly (> 100 °C prematurely) or excessive exothermic heat buildup.

Location: In thicker internal sections of the appliance. Boiling MMA at 100.8 °C turns gaseous, trapping translucent spherical bubbles.

Contraction / Pressure Porosity

Cause: Insufficient resin charge in the gypsum mold during packing or inadequate flask clamp tightening.

Appearance: Diffuse irregular intergranular microporosities giving the appliance a whitish, opaque look.

Anisotropy and Stair-Stepping

Cause: Additive layer nature with varying print platform angles (0°, 45°, 90°).

Consequence: Micro-steps (stair-stepping) between layers create micro-grooves where bacterial biofilm easily settles.

Leaching and Tissue Cytotoxicity

The release of unreacted **free Methyl Methacrylate (MMA)** or residual oligomers (UDMA/TEGDMA) into the oral cavity is the primary cause of direct tissue toxicity.

Associated Clinical Conditions:
  • Denture Stomatitis: Inflammation and widespread erythema of the palatal mucosa in contact with the base.
  • Burning Mouth Syndrome: Paresthetic sensation and painful burning induced by local chemical irritation.
  • Type IV Hypersensitivity: Delayed T-cell mediated allergic reactions caused by free monomers or heavy metal pigments.

Safety Comparison: HPHT milled PMMA drastically minimizes chemical leaching (<0.3% residual), representing the gold standard for allergic or immunocompromised patients.

Microbial Adhesion and Candida albicans

Prosthetic surface colonization by **Candida albicans** and subsequent biofilm formation correlate with micro-structural surface roughness and water sorption.

CAD/CAM Milled PMMA:

Extremely high molecular density prevents colonization and allows permanent, uniform mirror polishing.

Flask Resin:

Progressive erosion and exposure of surface micro-pores facilitate mechanical plaque retention over time.

3D Printing Resin:

The "stair-stepping" effect requires thorough mechanical finishing or dedicated photo-coating sealing to eliminate fungal micro-shelters.
